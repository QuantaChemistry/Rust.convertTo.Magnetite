Origin.Search: ["steam bluing" passivation](

quote:
>"Steam bluing (or steam treating) is a high-temperature passivation process where cleaned iron or steel parts are heated to 500–570°C (600–800°F) in a sealed vessel and exposed to superheated water vapor. This reaction forms a thin, tightly adherent blue-black magnetite (Fe₃O₄) conversion layer that provides mild corrosion resistance, increased surface hardness, and superior oil retention."
